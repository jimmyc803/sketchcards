## What it does

- **Draw cards.** Sketch from memory, then compare side by side or overlaid on the answer.
- **Spaced repetition.** Grade yourself Missed, Close or Got it, and each card comes back at the right time.
- **Practice mode.** Drill any deck without changing your schedule.
- **Made for iPad and Apple Pencil.** Pressure, palm rejection, colors, and lined or grid paper.
- **Syncs and works offline.** Build decks on a laptop, study on the go.

## Get started

1. Open **[sketchcards-mu.vercel.app](https://sketchcards-mu.vercel.app)** and sign in with Google.
2. On iPad or iPhone, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** to use it like an app.
3. Go through the Welcome deck, then make your own, or import a deck such as [`examples/amino-acids.json`](examples/amino-acids.json).
